About

This website lets you explore rankings for the top 100 NFL players. You can filter players by year, position, rank, and team to compare rankings across different seasons. This started as a fun side project that I had wanted to build for a while.

I don't own any of the data or images used on the site, and it is not affiliated with the NFL in any way. The statistics were sourced from Pro Football Reference, and player photos were gathered through Google searches. I manually reviewed each player and did my best to find a photo from the correct year. There is no monetization or advertising on the site, and no profite is being made.

One challenge was offensive linemen, since there is very little publicly available statistical data for them beyond games played and, in some cases, the occasional solo tackle.

Here is more information about the rankings: NFL Top 100 on Wikipedia. The rankings are voted on by the NFL players themselves, so this isn't my opinion. Feel free to reach out to me for any questions or feedback at t.manuel2095@gmail.com.
